Unit 2 Quick Reference Guide: VSEPR Shapes & IMFs

Use this reference chart to help determine 3D molecular geometry, polarity, and intermolecular forces.

1. VSEPR 3D Molecular Geometry Chart

Total Electron Domains (around Central Atom) Bonding Domains (Attached Atoms) Lone Pairs on Central Atom 3D VSEPR Shape Typical Polarity Rule
2 2 0 Linear Nonpolar if outer atoms are identical (e.g., $\text{CO}_2$); Polar if different.
3 3 0 Trigonal Planar Nonpolar if all 3 outer atoms are identical (BF3); Polar if outer atoms differ (like CH2O!).
4 4 0 Tetrahedral Nonpolar if all 4 outer atoms are identical (CH4); Polar if outer atoms differ.
4 3 1 Lone Pair Trigonal Pyramidal Always Polar due to the lone pair pushing bonds down asymmetrically (e.g., PCl3, NH3).
4 2 2 Lone Pairs Bent Always Polar due to asymmetrical lone pairs (e.g., H2O).

2. Intermolecular Forces (Strongest → Weakest)

  • 1. Ion-Dipole Attraction (Strongest): Between full ionic charges (Ca2+, Cl-) and partial charges (δ+, δ-) of polar water.
  • 2. Hydrogen Bonding: Special strong dipole attraction ONLY when H is directly covalently bonded to N, O, or F (e.g., Ethanol -OH, water H2O).
  • 3. Dipole-Dipole Forces: Between polar molecules that have permanent partial charges (δ+, δ-) without N-H, O-H, or F-H bonds.
  • 4. London Dispersion Forces (LDFs): Present in all molecules, and the ONLY IMF in nonpolar molecules (like hydrocarbons made only of C and H). More total electrons = stronger LDFs = higher boiling point!

3. Key Unit 2 Rules

  • "Like Dissolves Like": Polar & ionic substances dissolve in polar solvents (water). Nonpolar substances dissolve in nonpolar solvents (oil, limonene, isoprene).
  • Distillation vs. Electrolysis: Distillation uses heat to break intermolecular forces (IMFs) between molecules (physical change). Electrolysis uses electricity to break intramolecular covalent bonds inside molecules (chemical change).
  • Chromatography (Rf): With polar water as the mobile phase, a higher Rf means the dye traveled farther with the water, so the dye is more polar.
